Medication Dispenser

A portable automatic medicine dispenser equipped with a built-in alarm system and secure storage unit. Designed using computer-aided design (CAD) software, this device helps users consistently follow their prescribed medication schedules by providing timely reminders and safely storing their medicines.

Tourist Midlands app

A mobile app designed to celebrate and promote art and culture across the West Midlands.Mosaic Midlands helps users discover local events,exhibitions, and creative spaces through real-time listings and secure user interaction.To connect communities by making cultural exploration simple, inclusive, and inspiring.

Connect Four

A command-line version of the classic Connect Four game, built entirely in Python. The project focuses on creating an interactive, functional prototype that supports both single-player and two-player modes, following the official game rules.
