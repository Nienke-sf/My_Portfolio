What is the project about?

Med-Clock is a medication reminder and dispensing system designed to help users manage daily prescriptions safely and efficiently.

Aims & Objectives

  • Reduce missed medication doses
  • Provide clear alerts and reminders
  • Ensure ease of use for elderly users
