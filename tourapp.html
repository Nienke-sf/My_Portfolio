What is the project about?

Mosaic Midlands is a mobile app celebrating and promoting art and culture across the West Midlands. It aims to strengthen local engagement by providing up‑to‑date information about cultural events, exhibitions, and activities. The app targets a wide audience — from teenagers to adults, including local artists, cultural enthusiasts, tourists, and community members interested in exploring regional creative spaces.

the problem it helps to solve

Cuts in funding for cultural institutions have led to decreased visibility and participation in local events. Many community members remain unaware of ongoing cultural activities or how to access them. This lack of engagement threatens the sustainability of the West Midlands’ artistic identity and local creative economy.

Aims & Objectives

  • To promote engagement with West Midlands cultural events using mobile technology.
  • A user‑friendly application that provides clear, accessible event information.
  • Ensure inclusive design principles supporting accessibility and ease of use.
  • To integrate real‑time event data through a secure backend system.
  • To test, evaluate, and refine the app through iterative debugging for stability and performance.

Project Resources

  • - Firebase: Provided secure user authentication, data synchronization, and real‑time updates.
  • - Development Environment: Android Studio and Firebase SDKs for flexible development.
  • - Android Libraries: Supported accessibility, notifications, and content display.

Skills & What I Learned

Completing this project deepened my understanding of mobile UX design, backend integration, and app accessibility. I learned how to connect APIs, manage real‑time data, and troubleshoot dependency errors using Gradle. Implementing Firebase taught me about user protection, GDPR compliance, and the importance of strong database structure. I also gained valuable experience in planning realistic features — deciding against implementing Google Pay for ethical and time constraints — and learned how to adapt quickly when certain tools (like SQLite) didn’t align with the project’s needs. Most importantly, this project reinforced the value of user‑centred design: creating an app that feels intuitive, visually engaging, and culturally meaningful to its community.
