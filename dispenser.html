What is the project about?

This is a medication reminder and dispensing system designed to help users manage daily prescriptions safely and efficiently. This project was developed to empower individuals to better manage their health and well-being through accessible technology. By offering a smart, user-friendly solution, it will aim to reduce the risk of missed doses and negative health effects. The mission is to make a positive impact on people’s lives by combining thoughtful design with practical innovation.

the problem it helps to solve

Many individuals struggle to remember when to take their medication or may accidentally take it at the wrong time. Traditional reminders can be easy to ignore or lack controlled access to medication, increasing the risk of missed or incorrect doses.

Aims & Objectives

  • Reduce missed medication doses
  • Provide clear alerts and reminders
  • Ensure ease of use for elderly users

Project Resources

The system will be programmed using Arduino IDE and C++, allowing precise control of the timer, motor, alarm, LED, and LCD display. When the set time is reached, the Arduino triggers the alarm and LED while activating the motor to release the medication. Computer-Aided Design (CAD) will be used to create 3D models of the dispenser components, ensuring accurate visualization, fit, and functionality before manufacturing.

  • - LCD Display: Shows time and countdown.
  • - Alarm System: Buzzer and flashing LED for alerts.
  • - Motor & H-Bridge Driver: Operate the dispensing mechanism.
  • - Push Button & Breadboard: Support system testing and input controls

Skills & What I Learned

Throughout this project, I developed strong technical skills in both software and hardware integration. I learned how to program an Arduino using C++ to manage timing logic, hardware control, and system states, ensuring each component worked in sync. This deepened my understanding of how software commands translate into real-world actions through motors, LEDs, buzzers, and LCD displays. I also gained experience in handling system timing and event responses by applying timers and interrupts to trigger alarms, alerts, and dispensing actions with precision. Using simulation tools allowed me to test system behavior virtually before building the physical prototype, helping me identify and correct issues early on. In addition, I enhanced my skills in CAD design by creating functional 3D models that bridged the gap between digital concepts and physical assembly, giving me a clearer perspective on how mechanical and electronic design come together in embedded systems.

  • Arduino programming & hardware integration
  • Motor control and timing logic
  • User-focused system design
